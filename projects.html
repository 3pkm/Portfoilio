PROJECT #07 • SATELLITE REMOTE SENSING MARIDA BENCHMARK

Tidewatch: Ocean Debris & Metocean Drift Engine

Autonomous satellite remote sensing pipeline tackling marine plastic slicks under dense oceanic cloud cover (“The Clouds Rolled In”). Employs a 14-channel deep neural segmentation UNet on Sentinel-2 MSI multispectral imagery, a 500-particle Runge-Kutta 4 (RK4) stochastic metocean drift ensemble with KDE probability contours, and cloud-aware spatio-temporal tracking across orbital revisits.
